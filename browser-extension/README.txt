Send to NEXDROP: browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers

1. In NEXDROP open Settings > App and turn on "Open links sent from the browser".
2. In the browser open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge) and turn on Developer mode.
3. Choose "Load unpacked" and pick this browser-extension folder.

Then right-click a page, link, image or video and choose "Download ... with NEXDROP", or click the
NEXDROP button in the toolbar to send the page you are on. The first time, the browser asks whether
to open NEXDROP; tick "Always allow" to skip that question.

Links only land in NEXDROP's links box. Nothing downloads until you press Start. The extension has
no access to your pages' content and sends nothing anywhere except to NEXDROP on this computer.
